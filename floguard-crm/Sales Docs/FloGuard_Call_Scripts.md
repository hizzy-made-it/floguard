# FloGuard Call Scripts

## First call — homeowner (FrenchDrain / general)

**Opener (8 seconds):**
"Hi, is this [Name]? I'm [Rep] with FloGuard — we engineer drainage systems for homes here in [City]. After that last storm we've been walking a lot of yards in your neighborhood with standing water. Quick question — when it rains hard, how long does water sit in your yard?"

**Permission:** "Mind if I take 30 seconds to see if this is even worth your time?"

**Discovery (pick 2–3):**
- "After a hard rain, how long does the water stand?"
- "Has it reached the foundation, the lanai, or inside?"
- "What have you tried already — regrading, gutters, a trench?"
- "How long have you owned the home — any plans to sell?"

**Pitch (one sentence):**
"Based on what you're describing, that's exactly what an engineered French drain and sump system fixes — and the assessment that tells you what YOUR yard actually needs is free."

**Close:**
"We're in [City] Thursday and Friday — morning or afternoon better for a 30-minute walk-through?"

## Sump pump variant

Opener swap: "…we keep crawlspaces dry through Florida storm season. Does your crawlspace or garage ever smell musty after rain?"
Battery-backup line: "The storm that cuts your power is the same storm pushing groundwater at your foundation — that's why we spec battery backup."

## Maintenance call (existing system)

"You've got a sump system at the house, right? When did anyone last actually test it? Pumps fail silently — and the first storm of the season is a bad time to find out. We do a full storm-readiness check: pump test, basin, discharge, battery. Late spring slots are going — want me to grab you one?"

## Property manager (B2B)

"I'm [Rep] with FloGuard — we handle drainage for rental portfolios across Central Florida. Every wet season you get the same tenant calls: musty smell, flooded yard, standing water at move-out. I'm not asking you to commit to anything — let us walk your single worst property this week, free, and hand you a written scope you can forward to the owner. Which property gives you the most grief?"

## Voicemail (homeowner)

"Hi [Name], this is [Rep] with FloGuard here in Port Orange. With the storms we've had, we're doing free drainage assessments in [City] this week — if your yard's been holding water, I'd love to take a look. No cost, no obligation. [Number]. Again, [Number]. Thanks!"

## Follow-up text (after no-answer)

"Hi [Name] — [Rep] with FloGuard (drainage, Port Orange). We're doing free yard assessments in [City] this week after the storms. 30 minutes, no obligation — want a morning or afternoon slot? Reply STOP to opt out."

## Never say

- A firm system price ("it'll run you about $8,000") — the band + free assessment only.
- "Insurance will probably cover it."
- "This will flood-proof your house."
- Anything from the [CONFIRM WITH OWNER] list in Pricing & Offers.
