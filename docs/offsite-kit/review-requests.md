# FloGuard — Review Request Templates

Send the first ask the same day the job is finished and the yard looks good. Keep it personal: use the customer's first name and mention one real detail from the job.

**Google policy rules (do not break these):**
- No incentives of any kind (discounts, gift cards, raffles, "free maintenance visit") in exchange for a review.
- Ask every customer, not just the happy ones (no "review gating"). Do not ask only for 5-star reviews.
- Never write a review for a customer, and never post reviews from staff or family.
- Use the direct review link: `{{GBP_REVIEW_LINK}}` (GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy link).

Placeholders: `{{FIRST_NAME}}`, `{{CITY}}`, `{{GBP_REVIEW_LINK}}`.

## 3-touch follow-up cadence

Stop as soon as a review comes in. Never more than 3 touches.

| Touch | When | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 (job complete) | SMS | Job-type SMS above |
| 2 | Day 3 | Email | Job-type email above |
| 3 | Day 10 | SMS | "Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}, last nudge from me, no pressure at all. If you're happy with the work, a quick Google review helps: {{GBP_REVIEW_LINK}} Thanks! — Heath Daniel, Founder — FloGuard" |

**Replying to reviews:** reply to every review within 48 hours. Thank them by first name, mention the service and city in a natural way ("glad the French drain in Port Orange is keeping the yard dry"), and never share private job details. For negative reviews, reply once, calmly, and take it offline.
