## Q&A seed pairs

Post the question from a separate Google account, then answer from the business account.

1. **Q:** Do you offer free estimates?
   **A:** Yes. Every job starts with a free on-site drainage assessment. We check slope, soil, water table, and discharge options before giving a written quote.
2. **Q:** How much does a French drain cost in the Daytona Beach area?
   **A:** It depends on length, depth, soil, and where the water can legally go. Most residential French drain jobs fall in the low-to-mid thousands; we give an exact price after walking the lot.
3. **Q:** What areas do you serve?
   **A:** Port Orange, Daytona Beach, Ormond Beach, South Daytona, New Smyrna Beach, and the rest of Volusia County.
4. **Q:** Do I need a French drain or a sump pump?
   **A:** If your yard has enough fall to a legal outlet, a gravity French drain usually works. Flat lots or high water tables often need a sump pump. The assessment tells us which one fits.
5. **Q:** Do I need a permit for drainage work?
   **A:** Work on your own lot usually doesn't, but discharge into the street or right-of-way can need a city permit. We check local rules for your address before work starts.
6. **Q:** How long does a typical install take?
   **A:** Most residential French drain and yard drainage jobs take one to three days, depending on size and access. We restore the work area when we're done.
