6. **No fake AggregateRating / invented chatbot prices.**  
7. **Canonical:** `https://www.floguardfl.com` only.

## 5. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) system

### Principles

1. **Entity first:** consistent NAP, GBP, Bing Places, citations, real reviews.  
2. **Answer first:** first paragraph resolves the query; no “welcome to our site.”  
3. **Citability:** self-contained paragraphs (134–167 words), tables, clear ranges.  
4. **Proof:** before/after, process steps, warranty, local neighborhoods.  
5. **Crawl access:** AI bots allowed in production (Cloudflare today is the blocker).  
6. **`llms.txt`:** keep as entity map — not a ranking lever alone.
