# FloGuard Off-Page SEO & AEO Link Strategy

## Goals
- 30+ quality backlinks in 12 months to key pages (services, pillars, cities)
- Unlinked mentions for AEO entity building
- Local authority for pack rankings

## Tactics
1. Guest Posting: Pitch 1-2 per month to Florida home, real estate, construction blogs. Topics: cost guides, storm prep. Link to 1-2 pillars.
2. Resource Pages: Find "best contractors Florida" lists, suggest FloGuard.
3. HARO/Connectively: Monitor for drainage/flooding queries, respond as expert.
4. Local Partnerships: Link from chambers, realtors, home inspectors (mutual).
5. Case Studies: Publish on site, share with clients for backlinks/testimonials.
6. Directories: High-quality ones only (not link farms).

## AEO Bonus
Unlinked mentions help AI recognize brand as authority.

## Tracking
Spreadsheet columns: Source, URL, Anchor Text, Target Page, DA/DR, Date, Notes.
Tools: Google Search "link:floguardfl.com", free backlink checkers, or paid like Ahrefs.

Start with 5 outreach emails this week to local blogs.
