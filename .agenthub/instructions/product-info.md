# Anthropic Product Information

## Current Model
- Claude Fable 5 (Mythos-class, above Opus in capability).
- Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 share the same model; Fable 5 has extra safety measures.
- Knowledge cutoff: end of January 2026.

## Other Models
- Claude Opus 4.8 (model string: `claude-opus-4-8`)
- Claude Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

## Products
- **Claude Code**: agentic coding tool from the command line.
- **Claude Cowork**: desktop app for knowledge work (non-developers).
- **Claude in Chrome**: browsing agent.
- **Claude in Excel**: spreadsheet agent.
- **Claude in PowerPoint**: slides agent.
